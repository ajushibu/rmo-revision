# RMO Revision

Static PWA (no build step) for an Indian-trained doctor preparing to work as an RMO in Australia. Hash router in `app.js`, content in `data.js` and `recall.js`, study tools in `tools/`, case data in `tools/cases/`.

- Clinical content is Australia-first: check doses against Australian sources and say plainly what could not be verified.
- Refreshing on-call cases from the user's weak points: follow `CASES.md`.
- Bump the cache name in `sw.js` whenever files change, then push to `main` and confirm the Vercel deployment.
- Recall checklists (`recall.js`): every item has a section `g` (R recognise, A assess and investigate, T treat now, C complications and traps, F after and follow-up) and items are listed in the order you would think them through on the ward, not by importance. Mark must-know items with `p: 1`. Source `s: "k"` means written from the topic's Study Kit (name any Australian source inside `k`). Each card's `t` is a full question that its `k` fully answers (see "Card question rule" in `CASES.md`). Write `k` as short lines separated by `\n` (one idea per line, `Label: text` for a bold label, `→` for order or cause and effect, a line starting with `(` is a small note); never drop a number, dose or source note to make it shorter.
- Card context (`ctx`) and "Make this a case" requests (`caseReq`) sync in the state: process them with `scripts/requests.py` and the last section of `CASES.md`.
- Highlights saved in study tools and the user's 'what I missed' notes live in the synced state (`highlights`, `notes`). Turn highlights into recall cards with `CASES.md` (second half).
- Looks: `look.js` sets `data-look` (paper, night, classic) on the app and on every tool; app tokens live in `style.css`, tool tokens and the case-player layout in `tools/look.css`, case-player behaviour in `tools/shift-ui.js`. A new tool must load `../look.js` and `look.css` after its own styles (a `_shift.html` also loads `shift-ui.js` last), use the shared variable names (`--bg --surface --ink --muted --line --n-tint --display --body --mono`), and must not load web fonts. Add new tool files to `sw.js`.
- Case monitors can draw a simulated lead II strip: set `vit.rhythm` (keys and `+mods` are listed at the top of `tools/ecg.js`) on a case's start `vit` and on any option or step `vit` that changes the rhythm; the strip follows `vit.hr`. It is synthetic (built from textbook waveform rules, not recorded and not cardiologist-checked), so keep the caption saying so. New `*_shift.html` and `*_ward.html` pages load `ecg.js` after `../look.js`; old inline monitors need the `ecgTag(v)` call that `shift.js` has.
- Hard cases: orders signed in one step carry into the next as running orders, and the user must tap Keep or Stop on each. Keep on a `need` or `good` scores as if ordered; Stop on a `skip` or harm (X) earns a point, Keep on one loses a point; orders the step does not list are neutral either way. New orders file under five cue prompts (treat, tests, monitor, stop, calls; `bucket()` in `ward.js` maps catalogue categories). Plan answers are kept when the step's right answer is unchanged. Results show the score, up to three things to remember and the outcome, with the full list folded away. Write later steps so they only ask for what is new.
- Kindle: `kindle/rmorevision.koplugin` is a KOReader plugin (recall cards only). After changing `recall.js` or the topic list run `python3 scripts/kindle_export.py` (writes `kindle/cards.json`, copied into the plugin) and push; the Kindle's Update cards downloads it from the site. Ratings made on the Kindle sync through the `rmo_kindle_*` tables and functions in the shared Supabase project (`omuzlqabwlzhbrfipwqx`) and merge into `S.items` in `syncNow` (newest `t` wins). Scheduling rules are copied in `rmo_core.lua`; keep them in step with `rate()`/`nextStep()`/`levelOf()`/`queue()` in `app.js`. Four recall buttons: Again (rung 0, 1 day), Hard (same rung), Good (up one), Easy (up two); Good and Easy are refused before a card is due; old ratings `shaky` = again and `got` = good. Levels: new, weak (last answer Again), learning (rung 0 to 1), young (2 to 3), mature (rung 4). The Revise level filter is kept on the device only.

- "Where this is taught" links: `kitmap.js` (`window.KITMAP`) gives each recall card a target and an anchor: `c[id] = [target, anchor, buttons]`. Target `k:N` is kit step N, `k:p-x` a tab of the tabbed pe/af/brady kits, `t:N` tool N of the topic (a lab or picture). The anchor is the start of the line that answers the card; `kitFocus()` in app.js opens the tab, presses any listed buttons (the tachycardia four-box chooser), scrolls to the line and outlines it. Route `#/tool/<topic>/<tool>/k/<step or tab>/<rv|t>/<card id>`. Every card must point at a line that actually teaches it: when a kit does not teach a card, add a box to the right step (the box carries `data-card="<id>"` in html kits) rather than pointing at something nearby. When you add a card or change a kit step, update `KITMAP` and re-check that the anchor is still found and unique.
