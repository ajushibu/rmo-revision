The one rule for the kidney

Potassium is lost in the urine in large amounts only when both are up: aldosterone pulling sodium in, and plenty of sodium arriving. One dial alone gives little.

Three things to fix in your own notes

    When potassium will not correct

    Check magnesium. A low magnesium makes the kidney leak potassium, and the potassium stays low until the magnesium is replaced.
