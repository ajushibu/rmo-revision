How far to trust this. The four pillars and IV iron criteria come from the Australian consensus statement (Sindone et al, MJA, August 2022). The in-hospital caution limits and 1-2 week follow-up come from the 2026 Australian consensus on care after heart failure hospitalisation (Heart Lung Circ 2026). The 2 kg in 2 days advice is from NPS MedicineWise. Pulmonary oedema doses come from an undated Australian ED handbook (eMedSA). The diuresis timing, goals, resistance steps and device criteria come from the US template and were not found in Australian sources. Check eTG and your hospital protocol before you act.
