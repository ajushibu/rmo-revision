Tension first, then observe, aspirate or drain, then look after the tube. Press Next.

How far to trust this. Management of stable spontaneous pneumothorax is from the NSW ACI treatment-options page. The Australian needle site I could read is a paediatric guideline (Perth Children's Hospital: second intercostal space, mid-clavicular line, 14 or 16 gauge cannula, 90 degrees). The adult site is not verified here: use the site your hospital or trauma protocol teaches. The size threshold for a "large" pneumothorax is not on the page I read; use your unit's value.
