How far to trust this. Doses and steps marked Australia come from ANZCOR Guideline 11.9, whose page shows no revision date. ANZCOR gives no cardioversion energy, no sedation drug or dose, no lignocaine dose, and does not mention procainamide, so those come only from the US template or your hospital protocol. The advice to avoid amiodarone in torsades and verapamil or diltiazem in wide tachycardia is from ANZCOR. Always check eTG and your local protocol.
