Oxygen lab and ladder game

Learn how each device changes what the patient really breathes, then practise choosing the next rung.

Simplified for learning, not a clinical decision tool. This is a teaching model with rounded numbers. Targets (92 to 96%, or 88 to 92% for CO2 retainers) are from the TSANZ guideline as summarised in the MJA (2015). Device behaviour is standard teaching, not an Australian guideline: confirm with your hospital protocol and your senior.
